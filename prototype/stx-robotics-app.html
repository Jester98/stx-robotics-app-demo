App concept · South Texas Robotics

The STX Robotics app.

One app for families, students, mentors and staff. It's built in the same look as stxrobotics.com, so it feels like part of the brand.

Tap through it on the phone. Use the Parent / Staff switch at the top to see each side.

  • CampsRegister by community, with automatic waitlists
  • TeamsVEX V5 tournaments, matches and practice RSVPs
  • Prints3D print requests from queue to pickup
  • MentorsLog and approve student mentor hours
  • TextsReminders to families who opted in

Concept prototype. Everything except the Summer 2026 totals from stxrobotics.com is sample data: names, dates, team numbers and events. Nothing is saved or sent.
